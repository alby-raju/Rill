# Community conduct

Rill welcomes people of every background and experience level. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, Discussions and other spaces maintained for the project.

## Participation

- Be respectful, patient and constructive. Explain technical disagreements with evidence and focus criticism on the work.
- Welcome questions and newcomers. Respect people's time, boundaries, names and pronouns.
- Share synthetic examples and redact credentials, private endpoints and personal information.
- Stay on topic and respect maintainers' decisions about scope and supported behavior.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, doxxing and spam are unacceptable. Do not retaliate against someone who raises a concern or repost abusive/private material as evidence in a public thread.

## Reporting concerns

Use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** tools to report abuse privately to GitHub. Follow [GitHub's reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

For project moderation, you may use the [maintainer contact request form](https://github.com/alby-raju/Rill/issues/new?template=contact.yml) to ask for a private contact channel. Do not include names, allegations, screenshots, personal information or other sensitive details in that public request. Maintainers will arrange an appropriate follow-up; no dedicated email address or guaranteed response time is currently offered.

Report software vulnerabilities through [the security policy](../SECURITY.md). Do not put exploit details in a public conduct report or ordinary issue.

## Moderation

Maintainers may ask people to change their behavior, edit or remove content, lock a conversation, or restrict participation using GitHub's moderation tools. They will consider context and severity; serious or repeated abuse may result in removal without a prior warning. Maintainers are also expected to follow this policy. Concerns about a maintainer can be reported directly to GitHub through its reporting tools.
